# SiteRemade V15

No SQL migration is required.

## Test this exact flow
1. Open a client website with the SiteRemade widget installed.
2. Enter name/email/phone and send a website chat message.
3. Confirm a New lead appears and the Leads badge increments.
4. Open Inbox, take over the conversation, and reply as the business.
5. Leave the website chat open for up to 5 seconds; the business reply should appear there.
6. If Resend/Twilio are configured, verify the same business reply also reaches the lead by those channels.
7. Book an appointment for that lead and confirm the calendar card shows time + customer + appointment type/status.
8. Click the appointment and confirm its detail card can open the customer record.
